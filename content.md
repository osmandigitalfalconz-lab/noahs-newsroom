Four chapters (the investigation's own section headings, with their real
opening lines, trimmed):

1. 01 / The Consulting Problem — "The three firms listed on Brighter Futures
   LA's disclosure — Pacific Grove Associates, Civic Reach Strategies, and a
   company called simply Trellis — were each paid between $90,000 and
   $130,000 for services described as 'strategic communications consulting.'
   None of the three firms has a website."
2. 02 / Following the Money — "A PAC or campaign committee makes a large
   payment to a consulting firm. The consulting firm, in many cases, has no
   visible operations. The money enters the firm and does not reappear in any
   subsequent public filing."
3. 03 / The Wilshire Pipeline — "The mail drop on Wilshire Boulevard is
   registered as the business address for at least nine separate consulting
   entities that have received payments from political committees active in
   LA city elections over the past six years. Total payments to firms at that
   single address exceed $2.1 million."
4. 04 / What Oversight Looks Like — "The Ethics Commission publishes all
   filings online, which creates an appearance of transparency. The raw data
   is available. But the filings are designed to record transactions, not to
   explain them."
